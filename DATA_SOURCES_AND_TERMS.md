# Sources and use conditions

## Upstream corpus materials

The [UD English-ESL / TLE documentation](https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/en_esl/index.html) identifies the annotations as CC BY-SA 4.0 and explicitly distinguishes them from the underlying learner text, which must be obtained separately. The dependency diagnostics in this repository were calculated from the official lexicalised UD 2.3 release; that source archive is **not redistributed** here.

The [CLC FCE Dataset page](https://ilexir.co.uk/datasets/index.html) directs users to the authorised Cambridge download and its licence. The licence accompanying the local source dataset grants non-exclusive, non-transferable use for non-commercial research and education; it does not transfer ownership. It also requires acknowledgement of Yannakoudakis et al. (2011). This repository does not replace that licence or provide a new licence for the original dataset.

No original/corrected text, lexical tokens, FCE XML, individual CLC/E1–E4 score table, or original demographic-record table is included. Researchers needing those source records should obtain them from the authorised provider and review the applicable terms themselves.

## Numerical derivatives

This is a numerical research-output deposit. It supplies derived alignment counts, mismatch flags, proportions, model summaries, and reported aggregate descriptives. Learner and sentence identifiers have been replaced with release-specific pseudonyms, and no crosswalk is distributed.

No blanket MIT, CC0, or other unrestricted licence is applied to this mixed-provenance deposit. Publishing these numerical derivatives does not grant rights in upstream corpus materials or remove upstream use restrictions. Where annotation-derived material is reused or adapted, the applicable UD attribution/share-alike terms remain relevant. Consult the source providers for uses beyond non-commercial research and education or where rights are uncertain.
