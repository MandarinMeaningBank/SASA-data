# Known limitations and unavailable inputs

1. **Sentence-level SASA scores are outside the deposit scope.** No historical scoring files or replacement scores are included. Do not reconstruct individual observations from rounded manuscript summaries.
2. **Different annotation versions must not be conflated.** The dependency diagnostics use UD 2.3, whereas the historical provisional SASA implementation used UD 1.x annotations. These diagnostics do not exactly decompose or validate that implementation.
3. **The relation-table definition includes governor changes.** In the archived calculations, inconsistency means a label mismatch and/or an aligned-governor mismatch. A label-only description does not reproduce the supplied relation tables. Separate flag columns preserve the distinction.
4. **Alignment is conditional.** Only 63,497 of 84,010 reference dependencies enter the main comparable denominator. Unaligned dependents and unresolved governors are not classified automatically as errors or successes. Alignment sensitivity outputs are retained; none is a manually validated gold standard.
5. **Sparse relations and individual profiles are uncertain.** Missing exposure is not a zero rate. The adjusted models use relation-specific samples and exploratory linear approximations. Intervals are pointwise, not adjusted pairwise significance tests or evidence of L1 transfer mechanisms.
6. **No BERT/RoBERTa prediction files are supplied.** This dependency-diagnostic deposit cannot reproduce or validate Transformer correlation or significance comparisons. No new model experiment or significance test was run for this upload.
7. **Proficiency-model reproduction is incomplete.** Individual CLC/E1–E4 marks and the original identifier crosswalk are withheld from this public deposit. Adjusted estimates can be inspected but not independently refitted from these files alone. Obtain authorised source records and the appropriate additional inputs before claiming full reproduction.
8. **Sampling is limited.** These are TLE-selected paired sentences, not every sentence of each original FCE script. The 2000/2001 coverage counts describe this sample; they do not establish that all 71 scripts from 2001 have four examiner scores. L1 associations are descriptive, not causal or an L1 ranking.
